## How uncertainty is reported

Two distinct quantities are reported and never combined. 'bootstrap_ci_over_images' is a stratified bootstrap within a SINGLE seed and describes sampling variability over images from the same evaluated image distribution. The resampling unit is an image, not a patient: patient identity is unverified for this dataset. 'across_seeds' is the mean +/- s.d. of the three per-seed point estimates and describes training variability. The three seeds score identical images, so pooling them into one image-level interval would treat correlated predictions as independent.

Calibration: expected calibration error over 15 equal-width bins on [0,1] applied to the maximum predicted probability.

BRAIN-META: not attempted - per-image predictions unavailable.

## Limitations

- The `+/-` on aggregate rows is seed variation (n=3); the bracketed intervals are image-level bootstrap within one seed. They are different quantities and must not be quoted interchangeably.
- `external_full` mixes re-encoded copies of training images with genuinely unseen ones and therefore overstates cross-source performance. The `cross_source_disjoint` row is the zero-shot measurement.
- No comparison against BRAIN-META is made here; its per-image predictions are not available, so no significance test is possible.
- These metrics describe held-out images, not held-out patients. Patient-level independence is unverified and is the subject of Phase 2.
