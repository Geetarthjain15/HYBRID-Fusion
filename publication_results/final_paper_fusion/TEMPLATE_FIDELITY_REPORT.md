## Structure retained

The editable manuscript follows the reference article's broad MethodsX sequence: title and author block, highlights, graphical-abstract note, abstract, keywords, specifications table, background, method details, validation, comparison, implications, limitations, conclusion, declarations and numbered references. The proposed work adds dedicated internal and cross-source ablation sections because those are central evidence in this project.

## Formatting retained or approximated

- Single-column scientific layout with compact body text, hierarchical headings, restrained table rules and figures placed near the relevant section.
- A4 page, 20 mm margins, 10 pt Times New Roman body text, compact captions and editable Word tables.
- Figures supplied independently in PNG and PDF.

## Deliberate deviations

- Publisher logos, Elsevier branding, article-history metadata and typeset identifiers were not copied.
- The reference PDF embeds subsetted Charis SIL fonts. Charis SIL is not installed locally, so Times New Roman is used as an editable, widely available substitute. Exact publisher typography requires the journal's production template.
- The source PDF is a final publisher rendering, not an editable submission template; exact line breaks, kerning and pagination cannot be reproduced responsibly from the PDF alone.
- Author, ethics, funding, repository and venue fields remain placeholders rather than invented text.

The result is a publication-oriented editable draft, not a claim of pixel-identical publisher typesetting.
