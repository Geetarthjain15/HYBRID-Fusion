## Scope and limitations

- The s.d. reported here is across three training seeds only. Bootstrap confidence intervals over images are a separate quantity and are produced in Phase 1, not here.
- No comparison against BRAIN-META is made: its per-image predictions are not available in this repository.
- Files were hashed as found. The audit cannot attest to anything that happened before it first ran.
