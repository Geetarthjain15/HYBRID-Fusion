--------------------------------------------------
FAQ & DATASET INFO:
--------------------------------------------------
Q: Is the MRI dataset included in this directory?
A: No, raw image files are kept out to keep the workspace lightweight.

Q: Are model weights included?
A: Yes! The weights (vit_brain_tumor_5class.pth and hybrid_brain_tumor.pth)
   are present in the src/ directory and load automatically out-of-the-box.

Q: How do I train on a custom dataset?
A: Place your training images in dataset/Training/<category> and run:
   PYTHONPATH=src ./env/bin/python src/train.py
